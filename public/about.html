Background

I'm a Space Systems Engineer at L3Harris with over 7 years of engineering experience spanning the automotive and space industries. I hold an undergraduate degree in Aerospace Engineering and a Master's Degree in Space System Engineering, both from the University of Michigan.

At L3Harris, I develop and maintain multiple high-fidelity simulations — from replicating how data flows through a satellite bus to building environments for developing and testing detection algorithms. The work demands tight integration between systems engineering, software, and signal processing domains.

In addition to my engineering role, I served as IPT Lead for a sector of our Internal Research and Development (IR&D) group, where I led a team of 20 engineers through the design, development, and delivery of multiple algorithms. That experience sharpened how I approach cross-functional technical leadership under real program constraints.

Outside of Work

My free time is spent expanding my skills in the technical space — building websites, creating apps, and taking on hardware projects. I believe the best engineers stay curious beyond their primary domain. A complete list of personal projects can be found on the Projects page.
