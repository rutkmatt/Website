Background

I'm a Space Systems Engineer at L3Harris with over 8 years of engineering experience spanning the automotive and space industries. I hold an undergraduate degree in Aerospace Engineering and a Master's Degree in Space System Engineering, both from the University of Michigan.

At L3Harris, I develop and maintain multiple high-fidelity simulations — from replicating how data flows through a satellite bus to building environments for developing and testing algorithms. The work demands tight integration between systems engineering, software, and signal processing domains.

In addition to my engineering role, I spent time serving as CAM Lead for a sector of our Internal Research and Development (IRAD) group, where I led a team of 20 engineers through the design, development, and delivery of multiple algorithms. That experience sharpened how I approach cross-functional technical leadership under real program constraints.

Outside of Work

Outside of work, I'm most at home outdoors — hiking, biking, skiing, and sailing whenever the opportunity arises. When I'm off the trails, I channel that same energy into cooking and technology. In the kitchen, I enjoy experimenting with new recipes and refining old favorites — it's a creative outlet that also guarantees a great meal. On the technical side, I take a similar approach: continuously picking up new skills while deepening existing ones through personal projects spanning web development, application design, and hardware builds. I believe the key to a good life is a careful balance between exploring new skills while refining old ones. A full list of personal projects can be found on the Projects page.
