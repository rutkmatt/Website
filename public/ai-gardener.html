AI Gardener

A fully autonomous indoor herb garden powered by a Raspberry Pi, IR camera, and on-device AI — self-managing watering, lighting, and nutrients with weekly health reports.

Raspberry Pi IR Camera On-Device AI Python Computer Vision Embedded Systems
This project is currently under active development. Photos, hardware diagrams, and full documentation will be added as the build progresses.
Overview

What it does

Born from a passion for cooking and the challenge of maintaining fresh herbs amid a demanding schedule, this project combines hardware and AI to create a fully autonomous indoor herb garden system. Built on a Raspberry Pi with an IR camera and a lightweight on-device AI model, the system independently manages watering, lighting, and nutrient delivery. A generated weekly report provides a full health summary — covering plant condition, resource levels, and flagged areas for review.

Features

Key Capabilities

Autonomous Watering

Soil moisture sensors trigger precision watering on demand. AI determines schedule adjustments based on plant growth stage and ambient conditions.

Smart Lighting

Adaptive grow lighting schedules driven by AI inference on plant health state. Adjusts spectrum and intensity based on growth phase.

IR Plant Vision

IR camera images are processed by a lightweight on-device model to detect discoloration, wilting, pest damage, and growth anomalies.

Weekly Health Report

Auto-generated weekly summary covering plant condition, resource levels (water, nutrients, light hours), and flagged observations for review.

On-Device AI

All inference runs locally on the Raspberry Pi — no cloud dependency, no latency. Lightweight model optimized for edge deployment.

Nutrient Management

Automated nutrient dosing system monitors solution levels and triggers delivery when thresholds are met, tracked per plant and growth cycle.

Technical

Hardware & Software

Hardware

Raspberry Pi · IR Camera Module · Soil moisture sensors · Grow lights · Peristaltic pumps · Relay board

AI / ML

Lightweight computer vision model running on-device. Trained on plant health imagery for anomaly detection and growth staging.

Software

Python-based control loop. Sensor polling, actuator control, and AI inference managed by a lightweight scheduler on Raspberry Pi OS.

Reporting

Automated weekly PDF/email report generation. Aggregates sensor logs, AI observations, and resource consumption over the week.
