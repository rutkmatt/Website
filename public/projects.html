Hardware Project

AI Gardener. Born from a passion for cooking and the challenge of maintaining fresh herbs amid a demanding schedule, this project combines hardware and AI to create a fully autonomous indoor herb garden system. Built on a Raspberry Pi with an IR camera and a lightweight on-device AI model, the system independently manages watering, lighting, and nutrient delivery. A generated weekly report provides a full health summary — covering plant condition, resource levels, and flagged areas for review.

App Project

Recipe Manager. A full-stack personal project designed to bring organization and intelligence to home cooking. The application features a searchable recipe library — filterable by title, ingredient, or meal type — alongside a meal planning calendar that links directly to individual recipes. Recipes can be imported from websites or PDFs, and each recipe page includes ingredients with interactive check-off, step-by-step instructions, and nutritional information. The app integrates with Google Calendar for meal scheduling and automatically compiles a smart shopping list in Google Keep — intelligently filtering out staples and prompting the user to confirm what to add. Personal notes, images, and reviews can be attached to any recipe. Built to run on both Linux and Windows, with recipe storage supported via a personal server or Google Drive.
